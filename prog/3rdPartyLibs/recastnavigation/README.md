Vendored subset of Recast Navigation: the Recast, Detour, and DetourTileCache
modules, used for navmesh generation and pathfinding. DetourCrowd and the
demo/debug tools are not included.

Upstream: recastnavigation, https://github.com/recastnavigation/recastnavigation
(zlib license). Vendored version is untagged; DetourNavMesh.h reports
DT_NAVMESH_VERSION = 7.
