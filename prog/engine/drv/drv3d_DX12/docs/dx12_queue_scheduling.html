Xbox Series X|S

_TARGET_SCARLETT RDNA2 · ID3D12Device8 · no ESRAM · single DMA engine · RT + VRS tier2 · 3 frames

Frame submit sequence & sync points

Same 6 sync calls as PC/Xbox One. Key delta: the upload/readback Signal/Wait (steps 2, 3, 4) land on the same shared DMA queue (readBackQueue = uploadQueue), so upload and readback cannot overlap.
